Ikshanaa

The journey to know yourself
begins right where you are.

At any age. At any stage of life.

A residential spiritual Programme rooted in self-enquiry, introspection and inner discovery.

16–19 December 2026Shreejaashram, Khodala

Discover Ikshanaa ↓Enquire about the Programme

The question

Amidst all that life asks of you, have you taken time to know yourself?

Life takes each of us through different experiences, responsibilities, aspirations and challenges.

We spend much of our lives understanding the world around us, fulfilling expectations, and finding our way through changing circumstances. Yet, amidst it all, how often do we pause to understand ourselves?

Perhaps the most important journey is the one we have yet to begin — the journey within.

What is Ikshanaa?

A space to pause. A journey to discover.

Ikshanaa is an invitation to turn within.

Rooted in the spiritual wisdom and guidance of Shree Guru, Ikshanaa is a residential spiritual Programme offering an opportunity for self-enquiry, introspection, and a deeper understanding of oneself.

Away from the constant demands of everyday life, the Programme creates space to reflect on who you are, where you stand in life, and what truly matters to you.

Ikshanaa encourages you to explore these questions and arrive at your own understanding. The journey begins by learning to look within.

The experience

What might you discover when you begin to look within?

Ikshanaa invites participants to explore five dimensions of inner discovery.

Self-enquiry
Explore who you are beyond the roles and identities of everyday life.
Introspection
Reflect upon your experiences, thoughts, choices and patterns.
Inner awareness
Pay closer attention to your inner world.
Spiritual understanding
Explore deeper questions of life through Shree Guru’s wisdom and guidance.
Clarity of direction
Reflect on what truly matters and where you want your journey to lead.

An invitation for every stage of life

Life has many stages. The journey within has no age.

There is no prescribed age, stage, or circumstance to begin the journey of self-discovery.

Wherever life has brought you, whatever experiences have shaped you, the opportunity to understand yourself more deeply remains open.

You need not wait for life to change before you begin to understand yourself.

The journey can begin exactly where you are today.
