Rice Sowing at Land of Humanity

In the month of August, sadhaks at Shreejaashram Khodala partook in sowing rice crops in the paddy fields at Land of Humanity. It was a moment of them coming together and working with the villagers, cultivating the land like they cultivate their minds with sadhana everyday. The office goers, homemakers all came down to work on the fields. Rejoicing the unconditional love showered by nature in the form of fertile lands and bountiful rain.

80th Independence Day Celebration

Shreejaashram Khodala also saw the celebration of the 80th Independence day, with Sadhaks hoisting the flag in the middle of the valley and it could be seen from distant roads that snaked across the surrounding hills.

Kailash Yatra & Solidarity

At the end of August, tragedy struck during Kailash Yatra and countless lives were lost. Shree Guru spent some time with the sadhaks to convey the pain and grief of the Gharana over the loss of lives that were beloved to a Guru avastha. She shared the wisdom of how life goes on despite the pain and that for a Guru, the loss of every individual life is like losing a child they nurtured with themselves.

Shreejaashram Khodala stands in solidarity with the loved ones of every life that was lost to this tragedy and sends love to them.
